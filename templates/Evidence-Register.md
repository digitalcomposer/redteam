## Handling notes

- Hash evidence immediately after collection and after transfer.
- Store originals read-only; perform redaction on working copies.
- Minimize credentials, personal data, tokens, database rows, and unrelated customer content.
- Record each custody transfer, recipient, channel, time, and resulting digest.
