## Status values

- `Open` — still present or rollback pending.
- `Removed` — operator artifact removed.
- `Restored` — customer setting returned to recorded before-state.
- `Customer action` — cleanup assigned to the asset owner.
- `Accepted` — customer explicitly accepts the remaining change.

Do not close the engagement while an unexplained artifact remains open.
